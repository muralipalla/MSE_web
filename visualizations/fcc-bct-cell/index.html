Why FCC, not BCT?

FCC conventional-cell edges Alternative BCT cell BCT body-centre lattice point

The tetragonal axes follow FCC directions:

[100]t ∥ [110]γ [010]t ∥ [110]γ [001]t ∥ [001]γ
at = aγ/√2 ct = aγ ct/at = √2

This is a non-standard I-centred tetragonal setting of FCC at the special metric ct/at = √2. It is a re-description of the same lattice points, not a phase transformation. The FCC conventional cell displays the highest symmetry directly, so FCC is the standard Bravais-lattice classification.
