Compare the coordination cages

Each section uses the same host-atom radius, R. Select a section to load it in the shared viewer above.

01 · CN 4

Tetrahedral void

Four touching host atoms occupy the vertices of a regular tetrahedron. The centre-to-vertex distance is √6R/2.

r/R = 0.225

02 · CN 6

Octahedral void

Six host atoms sit at the vertices of a regular octahedron. The centre-to-vertex distance is √2R.

r/R = 0.414

03 · CN 8

Cube-centre void

Eight touching host atoms occupy the corners of a cube. Half its body diagonal gives the centre-to-corner distance, √3R.

r/R = 0.732
