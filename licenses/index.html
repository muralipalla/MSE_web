Phase-diagram references

  • Cu–Ni: Diagramme phase Cu Ni by Romary, Wikimedia Commons, used under CC BY 2.5. The teaching diagram translates the labels, digitizes the phase boundaries as B-splines, and normalizes them to pure-component melting points.
  • Pb–Sn: Faze-Sn-Pb by Serych, Wikimedia Commons, released into the public domain. The teaching diagram reverses the source’s wt% Pb axis to wt% Sn, translates the labels, digitizes six boundaries as B-splines, and normalizes them to the lesson’s eutectic landmarks.
  • Fe–Fe3C: the B-spline geometry was traced from a reference supplied by the site author; that reference background is not redistributed in the website.
