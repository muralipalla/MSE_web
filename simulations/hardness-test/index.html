Calculate hardness and uncertainty

Complete three verified readings to unlock the numerical analysis.

1. Recalculate the hardness of reading 1 from its recorded dimensions.

Use the method equation and the mean of d1 and d2.

2. Calculate the arithmetic mean hardness.

H̄ = ΣHi / n

3. Calculate the sample standard deviation.

s = √[Σ(Hi − H̄)2 / (n − 1)]

4. Calculate the standard uncertainty of the mean from repeatability.

uA = s / √n

5. Calculate the two-sided 95% expanded repeatability uncertainty.

U95 = t0.975,n−1uA

Scientific basis

Equations, standards, and limitations

Brinell

A tungsten-carbide ball is pressed into the surface. Two perpendicular residual diameters are averaged, then HBW is calculated from load, ball diameter, and the spherical indentation area.

HBW = 2P / [πD(D − √(D2 − d2))]

Vickers

A 136° square-based diamond pyramid creates a four-sided impression. The two diagonals are averaged before the Vickers value is calculated.

HV = 1.8544P / d2

Sources and representative specimen data
  • ASTM E10-23 defines Brinell testing of metallic materials and includes an uncertainty appendix.
  • ASTM E92-23 covers Vickers and Knoop hardness testing of metallic materials.
  • Buehler Brinell guidance describes ball sizes, loads, dwell, perpendicular diameter measurements, and the HB equation.
  • Struers Vickers guidance documents the 136° diamond, optical diagonal measurement, load ranges, and HV equation.
  • Representative presets draw on Hydro 6061-T6 aluminium data, Copper Development Association C93600 data, Uddeholm Formax data, and NIST microhardness reference materials.
  • The small-sample interval uses the Student-t form described in the NIST/SEMATECH statistical handbook.

This is an illustrative teaching experiment, not a conformity test. The reported uncertainty describes repeatability of this small virtual sample only; it omits force, indenter, microscope calibration, surface preparation, thickness, spacing, and other systematic contributions. Consult the current purchased standard for laboratory work.
