The specimen series uses amplitudes from 170 to 340 MPa in 5 MPa steps. Transfer the nearest setting from the rig.

How the rig sets the stress

The yoke divides the hanging force F equally between two collars. Each force acts over 40 mm to produce a bending moment of M = Fℓ/2 in the gauge section. For gauge diameter d, the surface stress amplitude is σa = 32M/(πd3).

Measure fatigue life

Test smooth, normalized SAE 1045 steel specimens at R = −1. Repeat an amplitude to see how life varies, or run a series of four specimens at each of six amplitudes.

Select an amplitude and test a specimen.

Use fractures at two or more distinct amplitudes to fit the line. Refit after adding specimens.

Simulation model

Each specimen has simulated scatter around a Basquin stress–life curve. The standard deviation of log10 life is 0.08. Fitting only fractures can underestimate life near the stop because some longer-lived specimens are runouts.
