Read the construction

One diffraction event, three relationships

Real → reciprocal

A family of planes spaced by dhk maps to a reciprocal vector normal to those planes with |Ghk| = 1/dhk.

Elastic scattering

The incident and diffracted wavevectors have the same length: |ki| = |kf| = 1/λ. Their tips therefore lie on one circle.

Diffraction condition

When a reciprocal point touches the Ewald circle, q = kf − ki equals Ghk, which is equivalent to Bragg’s law.
