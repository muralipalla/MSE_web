Scientific basis

Pattern assumptions and sources

Peak positions are calculated from the displayed lattice parameters and Bragg’s law. Relative intensities are representative powder values; Gaussian broadening, background, and noise are added for an illustrative scan.

  • The α-Fe reference pattern follows NBS Circular 539, Volume 4; Cu and Mg follow NBS Circular 539, Volume 1.
  • Silicon uses the NIST SRM 640g powder lattice parameter at 22.5 °C from NIST Special Publication 260-245.
  • Bragg’s law and intensity/structure-factor interpretation follow the IUCr Online Dictionary of Crystallography and the IUCr educational article on powder-pattern intensity.

This model uses monochromatic Cu Kα1 and does not simulate the Kα2 line. Intensities are not suitable for quantitative phase analysis, texture measurement, or instrument calibration.
