Calculate and assess KQ

The secant has 95% of the initial elastic slope. These records rise smoothly to the secant intersection, so its force is FQ. The secant is a prescribed evaluation of the force record. Microscopic crack extension may begin before that point. Records with earlier peaks need the full standard’s interpretation rules.

From precrack to a qualified test result
  1. Choose an applicable test method, specimen geometry, orientation, and temperature. Machine the starter notch and grow a sharp fatigue precrack within the method’s prescribed limits.
  2. Measure the specimen dimensions, align the fixture, and load at the specified rate while recording force and displacement.
  3. Examine the exposed fracture surface to measure the initial fatigue-crack front and check its geometry. Use that measured crack length in the geometry calibration.
  4. Determine FQ from the force record and calculate KQ. Assess the force ratio, ligament size, and the remaining requirements before reporting KIc.

This lab supplies the initial crack length and illustrates the force-ratio and ligament checks using synthetic records.

Geometry factors and units

Use FQ in newtons and dimensions in metres, then divide the result in Pa√m by 106 for MPa√m. Let x = a/W.

C(T) KQ = [FQ/(B√W)] f(x)

f(x) = (2 + x)(0.886 + 4.64x − 13.32x2 + 14.72x3 − 5.6x4)/(1 − x)3/2

SE(B) KQ = [FQS/(BW3/2)] f(x)

f(x) = 3√x[1.99 − x(1 − x)(2.15 − 3.93x + 2.7x2)]/[2(1 + 2x)(1 − x)3/2]

S is the support span in the three-point bend fixture and equals 4W in this experiment. The synthetic records give the same KQ so you can compare the effect of size and record shape on the qualification checks.

Choose the test method

  • Predominantly linear elastic response under high constraint. ASTM E399 evaluates Mode I plane-strain fracture toughness with its prescribed validity checks. It directs cleavage-fracture testing to E1921 or E1820. ASTM E399
  • Appreciable plastic deformation or stable tearing. ASTM E1820 evaluates Mode I fracture resistance using J and crack-tip opening displacement (CTOD), including resistance curves. ASTM E1820
  • Ferritic steel undergoing cleavage in the transition range. ASTM E1921 uses a set of toughness tests and statistical analysis to determine the reference temperature T0, within its material and test limits. Its procedure builds on E1820 with additional requirements. ASTM E1921
  • Quasistatic testing of metallic materials. ISO 12135 provides methods using K, J, CTOD, and resistance curves for precracked specimens. ISO 12135:2021
