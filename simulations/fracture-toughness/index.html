Geometry factors and units

Use PQ in newtons and dimensions in metres, then divide the result in Pa√m by 106 for MPa√m. Let x = a/W.

C(T) KQ = [PQ/(B√W)] f(x)

f(x) = (2 + x)(0.886 + 4.64x − 13.32x2 + 14.72x3 − 5.6x4)/(1 − x)3/2

SE(B) KQ = [PQS/(BW3/2)] f(x)

f(x) = 3√x[1.99 − x(1 − x)(2.15 − 3.93x + 2.7x2)]/[2(1 + 2x)(1 − x)3/2]

S is the support span in the three-point bend fixture and equals 4W in this experiment. The synthetic records give the same KQ so you can compare the effect of size and record shape on the qualification checks.

Test standards

Use ASTM E399 for linear-elastic plane-strain fracture toughness. ASTM E1820 covers fracture resistance using J and crack-tip opening displacement (CTOD), among other Mode I parameters. ISO 12135 covers quasistatic fracture toughness in terms of K, J and CTOD. ASTM E399-24 · ASTM E1820 · ISO 12135:2021
