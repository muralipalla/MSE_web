Visualization of Structures

A chemical formula tells us which atoms are present. A structure tells us where they sit, which neighbours surround them, and how the pattern repeats. Explore representative structures from metals, ceramics, carbon nanostructures, layered solids, clays, and polymers in one interactive atlas.

This is an expandable teaching atlas, not an exhaustive catalogue of every known crystal, nanostructure, molecule, or polymer morphology.

Before you rotate

Read crystalline structures as lattice + basis

1

Lattice

The repeating translations define the Bravais lattice and the unit-cell geometry.

2

Basis

One or more atoms attached to each lattice point create the crystal structure.

3

Coordination

Nearest neighbours reveal local geometry; guide lines are not always literal covalent bonds.

Lower-dimensional carbon structures also need care: graphene is a two-dimensional lattice, nanotubes are rolled quasi-one-dimensional sheets, and C60 is a molecule rather than a repeating crystal. Polymer views use connectivity, conformation, architecture, and morphology; an amorphous coil or cross-linked network does not imply a repeating unit cell.

Structure is more than shape

Three distinctions worth remembering

Why is CsCl not BCC?

A translation by (½,½,½) exchanges Cs and Cl. Because unlike atoms are not translation-equivalent, CsCl has a primitive-cubic Bravais lattice with a two-species basis—not a BCC Bravais lattice.

Why is zinc blende not diamond?

Both have the same tetrahedral network topology, but diamond uses one element while zinc blende alternates Zn and S. The basis changes the symmetry.

Does a formula determine a structure?

No. AB2 and A2B3 are compositions, not structures. CaF2 and α-Al2O3 are representative prototypes among many possible polymorphs.

Included in this atlas

From periodic unit cells to material architecture

MET

Elements and common lattices

Simple cubic, BCC, FCC, HCP, and diamond cubic.

C

Carbon nanostructures and molecules

Graphene, an armchair SWCNT, a three-wall MWCNT, and buckminsterfullerene C60.

AB

Binary compounds

NaCl rock salt, CsCl, zinc-blende ZnS, and wurtzite ZnS.

CER

Complex ceramics

Fluorite, rutile, corundum, cubic perovskite, and layered YBCO.

LAY

Layered and clay structures

AB-stacked graphite, kaolinite 1:1 layers, and smectite 2:1 layers with an interlayer region.

POL

Polymer conformation and architecture

Linear, random-coil, branched, cross-linked, and folded-chain semicrystalline models.

Crystal prototypes are labelled as crystallographic models. Nanocarbon sheets and tubes are shown as finite idealized fragments, C60 is an isolated molecular cage, and clay and polymer views are deliberately labelled schematic when a broad family name does not specify one unique unit cell.
