Dislocations

Follow slip from local atomic rearrangement to the collective motion of curved dislocation lines. The two views connect lattice resistance at the core with the multiplication and interaction processes that govern plastic flow.

Section 01 · Core-scale view

Watch slip advance through the lattice

Compare edge and screw character, switch between perspective and orthographic projection, and reveal the slipped and unslipped regions. The adjacent Peierls plot tracks the periodic energy barrier and lattice-resistance stress as the core advances.

Section 02 · Network-scale view

Treat dislocations as interacting lines

Remove the atoms and follow a larger population of curved line segments. Inspect an authentic relaxed ParaDiS ensemble, then play a solver-derived Frank–Read sequence that bows out, multiplies, collides, and remeshes under applied stress.

EnsembleDense ParaDiS networksRepresentative relaxed configurations, not a movie trajectory.
SourceFrank–Read multiplicationA pinned line bows out and creates additional line length.
InteractionCollisions and remeshingA lecture-scale bridge to discrete dislocation dynamics.
