A finer SDAS can reduce segregation distance and refine interdendritic features. SDAS is not grain size, so this is not a direct Hall–Petch effect.

Show the SDAS relation and calibration

λ2/λ2,ref = (Ṫ/Ṫref)−n

This teaching calibration uses λ2,ref = 50 µm at Ṫref = 1 K s−1 and n = 0.33. Alloy chemistry, local solidification time, GL, interface speed, convection, anisotropy, and coarsening also affect spacing. Do not extrapolate one calibration across unrelated casting and additive-manufacturing regimes.

Model scope and references

The dendrite simulation is a deterministic visual growth model, not a solved phase-field or quantitatively calibrated cellular automaton. The steel diagrams use separate schematic TTT and CCT curve families. Product fractions are generated only to explain path logic and sum to 100%; they are not metallurgical predictions.

Louhenkilpi et al. (2006), dendritic solidification modelling · Alvarez et al. (2022), cooling rate and SDAS · Koistinen & Marburger (1959)
