Phase Transformations

A stable phase does not appear all at once. A thermodynamic driving force must overcome an interfacial barrier; atoms must move, interfaces must remain stable, and the growing structure must compete for space.

Follow one causal chain: driving force → nuclei → kinetics → interface → microstructure.

Model scope and learning sequence

The simulations are instructional models, not sources of design, casting, or heat-treatment data. Every submodule states its assumptions alongside the model. Mathematical derivations are collapsed by default so the primary route stays visual and conceptual.

The sequence emphasises solutal dendrites in engineering alloys and deliberately leaves melt convection, macrosegregation, and full phase-field mathematics outside this first module.
