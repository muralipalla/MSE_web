With τ = T/Tm, a schematic log rate is ln(J/J00) = −A/[τ(1−τ)2] − q/τ. Absolute rate needs calibrated site density, Zeldovich factor, and attachment frequency.

Unit-safe JMAK form

X(t) = 1 − exp[−ln2(t/t50)n]

The isothermal model assumes a homogeneous medium, prescribed nucleation and growth laws, and random impingement. One fitted n does not uniquely identify a mechanism.

Mobility comes from diffusion

The attachment term is the same thermally activated atomic motion explored through random walks, Fick’s laws, and kinetic Monte Carlo.
