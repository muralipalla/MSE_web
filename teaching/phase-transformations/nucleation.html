Classical-model limits and references

CNT becomes questionable for atomic-scale clusters, anisotropic or faceted nuclei, diffuse interfaces, strain-energy-dominated solid-state precipitation, and nonclassical pathways. Heterogeneous nucleation also depends on substrate size, roughness, lattice mismatch, chemistry, line tension, and active-site density. At r = r*, the embryo is at an unstable maximum—not a stable state.

Turnbull & Fisher (1949) · Turnbull (1950) · Fletcher (1958)
