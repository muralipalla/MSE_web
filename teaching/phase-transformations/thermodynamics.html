Experiment 1 · G–T

Undercooling separates GS from GL

Liquid and solid have equal Gibbs free energy at Tm. Below Tm, GS is lower. Near Tm, the separation grows approximately in proportion to ΔT = Tm − T.

Show the near-Tm derivation and sign convention

For L → S, define ΔGm = GS − GL. With constant enthalpy of fusion, linearisation near Tm gives

ΔGm ≈ −ΔHf,m(Tm − T) / Tm,   Δgv = ΔGm / Vm.

Δgv is negative below Tm. The positive quantity |Δgv| is labelled driving-force magnitude. For ΔT/Tm much above 0.1, heat-capacity corrections become increasingly important.

At T = TmΔGm = 0

Liquid and solid coexist in equilibrium.

Below TmΔGm < 0

Solidification lowers bulk free energy.

Above TmΔGm > 0

Melting is favoured instead.
