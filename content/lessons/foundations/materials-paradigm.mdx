# The Materials Paradigm

Materials engineering connects four ideas: processing, structure, properties,
and performance. A processing route changes structure across several length
scales; that structure controls observable properties; those properties determine
whether a component performs safely and efficiently in service.

## Worked example

Compare a rapidly cooled steel with the same composition cooled slowly. The
different cooling paths produce different microstructures and therefore different
strength, hardness, and toughness.

## Common misconception

Composition alone does not uniquely determine properties. Processing history and
microstructure are also essential.
