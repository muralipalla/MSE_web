Section 2

The materials science map

This four-part relationship can organize every future lesson, simulation, and quiz.

01

Processing

How a material is made, shaped, heated, cooled, or treated.

02

Structure

Arrangement from electrons and atoms to grains and phases.

03

Properties

Mechanical, thermal, electrical, magnetic, and optical response.

04

Performance

How well the material meets the needs of an application.
