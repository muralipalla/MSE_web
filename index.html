Section 1

Core Modules

Follow the lessons from foundational ideas to atomic bonding, crystal structures, close packing, crystal defects, phase equilibria, and microstructure.

1. Structure Property correlations

Understand the processing-structure-properties-performance relationship.

2. Strength from Interatomic Bonds

Separate and shear atomic planes to estimate ideal tensile strength, shear strength, modulus, and surface energy.

3. Crystal Structures

Rotate representative material structures, compare unit cells and coordination, and build HCP or FCC one close-packed layer at a time.

4. Crystal Defects

Explore point defects, interstitial voids, ionic disorder, grain boundaries, and ASTM grain size.

5. Phase Diagrams

Read water and alloy phase maps, construct tie lines, apply the lever rule, and connect Pb–Sn and Fe–Fe3C regions to microstructure.
